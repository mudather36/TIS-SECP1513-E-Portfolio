## Reflection: Industry Talk

### What I Learned
The industry talk introduced how PPG uses Digital IT to support its global operations.
The speakers explained the role of Data Analytics, IT Infrastructure, and SAP in managing
business processes efficiently. Through real examples, I learned how information systems
connect technology with business needs and decision-making in large organizations.
This session also highlighted the importance of having both technical knowledge and
problem-solving skills for a future career in IT.

### Suggested Improvements
One improvement would be to include more interactive discussions or longer Q&A sessions.
This would allow students to ask more questions and better understand real challenges faced
by IT professionals in the industry.

### Personal Reflection
Overall, the industry talk increased my awareness of how important IT systems are in modern
organizations. It motivated me to learn more about enterprise systems and develop skills
that are relevant to future IT careers.
